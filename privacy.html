Deadspan: Outpost privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Deadspan: Outpost is a free tower-defence game from Deadspan Arcade that runs in Telegram (as a Mini App) or in any web browser. We keep data to the minimum needed for the game to work.

What we store

  • Game progress: your night reached, coins, upgrades, best turret, daily-reward streak and sound setting. These are stored on your device (browser localStorage) and, when you play inside Telegram, in Telegram CloudStorage so your progress follows you between devices. CloudStorage data is held by Telegram and tied to your Telegram account.
  • Telegram user ID: when you play inside Telegram, the app can read your numeric Telegram user ID, which Telegram provides. It is used only to build your personal invite link (for example ?startapp=ref_123456) and, once the shop opens, to credit Telegram Stars purchases to the right player.

What we don't do

  • We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or location.
  • We do not use cookies or third-party analytics trackers in this version.
  • We do not sell your data.

Advertising (when enabled)

Optional "watch an ad" buttons (to double coins, rebuild the wall or double a daily reward) may be provided by AdsGram inside Telegram. Ads only play when you tap one of these buttons. AdsGram processes data under its own privacy policy. We will update this page before ads are switched on.

Purchases (when enabled)

Optional purchases use Telegram Stars, handled entirely by Telegram. We receive your Telegram user ID and the item bought so we can deliver it. Every item has fixed, stated contents, with no random or loot-box rewards.

Children

Deadspan contains cartoon zombie violence and is intended for players aged 13 and over, in line with Telegram's own age requirements.

Deleting your data

To delete your local progress, clear the site data in your browser or Telegram. To remove CloudStorage data, contact us via the game's Telegram bot and we'll explain how.

Contact

Message the Deadspan Telegram bot, or open an issue on the game's GitHub repository.
